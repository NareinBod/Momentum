# Three-page report blueprint

## 1. Executive operations overview
Top row: Net Sales, Gross Profit, Gross Margin %, Average Inventory Turnover, Products At Stockout Risk. Main canvas: monthly net sales and gross profit trend; category margin bar chart; stockout-risk table ordered by days to stockout. Add category and channel slicers.

## 2. Supplier performance & risk
Top row: Average Supplier Risk, High Risk Suppliers, average late delivery rate, average defect rate. Main canvas: supplier risk score horizontal bars with risk-band color; late-delivery vs. defect-rate scatter; supplier detail matrix. Add region slicer.

## 3. Demand & reorder planning
Top row: 30-day forecast units, products at stockout risk, median days to stockout. Main canvas: ranked forecast units by SKU; projected inventory exception matrix. Create What-If parameters `Reorder Quantity` (25–400, step 5) and `Lead Time Days` (5–60, step 1), and place them beside an exception table.

Design direction: restrained white canvas, charcoal typography, deep navy section labels, and green/amber/red used only for status. Use Segoe UI, 12px body text, and ample whitespace.
